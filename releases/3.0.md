Wave 3 brings Terran, Protoss and Zerg together in a playable Starcraft tabletop setup for Warhall.

- Units, weapon profiles, abilities and cards through Wave 3, with clickable rules and weapon keywords.
- Six official map layouts, deployment markers, coloured entry borders and an optional three-inch grid.
- Textured unit models with blue Terran and gold Protoss colours, original Zerg colours, and improved vehicle and flying-model sizes.
- Separate activation, faction, buff/debuff and status tokens, including inverted faction colours for two players using the same faction.
- Clearer unit profiles, with full weapon names, readable Target/Surge values and quick Devastating Charge summaries.

## Known limitations

- Units cannot yet stand on imported ramps/platforms. The ramp is temporarily compressed to about 16.7 mm so models on the ground remain more visible.
- Custom token and terrain menu previews can be missing because of Warhall client limitations.
- Map and deployment flipping can produce incorrect results. Check placement manually.
- Leading-model formation movement is a proposed Warhall feature and is not included.
